New York, New York, USA

History

New York City was founded by Dutch colonists in the early 1600s under the name New Amsterdam before the English took control and renamed it New York in 1664. It grew from a small trading post at the tip of Manhattan into one of the busiest ports and most influential cities in the world. Waves of immigration through the 19th and 20th centuries, especially through Ellis Island, shaped the city's neighborhoods and gave it its reputation as a melting pot of cultures. Today it stands as a global center for finance, media, fashion, and the arts.

Things to Do in New York

  1. Walk through Central Park and take in the skyline views.
  2. See a Broadway show in the Theater District.
  3. Explore Times Square and grab a bite in Hell's Kitchen.
